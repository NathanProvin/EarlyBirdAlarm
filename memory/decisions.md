# Early Bird Alarm — Decisions & Rules

## Concept
An alarm clock (Android first, iOS later). Each ring plays a different bird song or call. The user guesses the bird (1 of 4) to dismiss the alarm, then sees the answer.

## Guiding rules (apply to every task)
- **Minimalistic: less is more.** Every screen, setting, and element must earn its place. When in doubt, leave it out.
- **Simple: no overengineering or overcomplication.** Use the smallest solution that works. No abstractions, options, or infrastructure "for later". Use platform and package features before custom code.
- **Iterative design.** Build a PoC first with placeholders (a few bundled birds, placeholder images, a rough UI), and prove the core loop on a real device. Then add content and polish one iteration at a time.

## Card collection (TCG) — decided 2026-10-27
Goal: make the game shareable and give a reason to keep waking up with it ("gotta catch 'em all").
- **Earning:** a **correct guess** adds the bird's card to the collection. A wrong guess leaves a grey "heard" silhouette that a later correct guess turns into the card.
- **Rarity, 5 tiers**, one scale used everywhere (card color, which birds ring, weights). Based on the species' share of GBIF bird records in the cell, in its best month (thresholds tuned from the report):

  | Tier | Color | Card odds Easy | Medium | Hard |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Common | stone `#8C857B` | 60 % | 45 % | 25 % |
  | Uncommon | fern `#4F8F5B` | 32 % | 32 % | 34 % |
  | Rare | sky `#3B7DB8` | 7 % | 16 % | 27 % |
  | Epic | plum `#8A4FB0` | 1 % | 6 % | 13 % |
  | Legendary | gold `#D9A21B` | 0 % | 1 % | 3 % |

  **Two independent draws (decided 2026-10-30, `app/lib/birds.dart`):**
  1. **The bird** (when an alarm is set, or a booster opened): among birds around this month (any month with "All birds"), weighted by how common each is *this month* in the region — **16 / 9 / 6 / 3 / 2** from Common to Legendary (scarce birds a little more often than the first 16/8/4/2/1) — and limited by difficulty: Easy drafts Common/Uncommon species, Medium adds Rare, Hard all. Only songs on the phone, avoiding the 10 most recent birds.
  2. **The card's rarity** (when the answer is right): drawn from the table above for the current difficulty, **tilted toward the bird's scarcity this month** (`rarityTilt` = 2, decided 2026-10-30): each rarity's odds are halved for every tier between it and the bird's tier. At Hard a common bird gives C 49 · U 34 · R 13 · E 3 · L 0.4 %, a legendary bird C 7 · U 19 · R 31 · E 29 · L 14 % — no more Common Yellow-browed Warbler then Legendary Blackbird. Overall the mix stays close to the table (Hard ≈ 22/36/28/11/2: Epic/Legendary a bit scarcer, since most birds heard are common). A Blackbird can still come as a Legendary copy, just rarely. Rarity is the frame colour and the reveal sparkles only; stats roll as before.

  Consequences: rarity belongs to each card, not to the species. The Species view and "best copy" use your rarest copy (then the best quality); undiscovered "?" tiles have no rarity (the rarity filter hides them); Legendary hunter = own 5 Legendary cards. Cards won before 2026-10-30 keep the species tier they were won with.
- **Stats (4):** Weight (g), Wingspan (cm), Length (cm), Flight distance (km).
  - Sources: weight from AVONET (CC BY 4.0, credited); wingspan and length from Wikidata (CC0), missing values estimated from AVONET wing length and mass by a fit on species that have both; flight distance = distance between the species' January and June GBIF occurrence centroids (≈0 for sedentary birds).
  - Each card rolls each stat around the **species mean** (spread: real AVONET spread where available, otherwise a fixed coefficient of variation per stat), rolled once when the card is earned, never changed. **Since 2026-10-30, a fat upward tail:** z = 0.8 × a "fine specimen" factor shared by the card (Student-t, 3 df, upward; narrow normal × 0.35 downward) + 0.6 × the stat's own normal noise, clamped to ±3σ. Quality ≥ 80 % for ~8.5 % of cards (was 1.7 %), ≥ 95 % for ~1.7 % (was ~0), ≤ 20 % for ~1 % (was 1.7 %).
  - **Card stock (2026-10-30):** a won card's paper is tinted with its rarity (5 %) and printed with a pattern per rarity, **all equally faint** (lines 12 %): **guilloche** (interlaced waves, banknote-style) for Common and Uncommon, a coarse **paper grain** for Rare (was a diamond lattice until 2026-11-01), a blurred **foliage shadow** (44 leaves, as under a branch) for Epic, a golden **camouflage** (irregular patches in light, gold and shadow shades) for Legendary. No sheen (tried, too visible); feather quills tried for Epic, replaced. "Heard" cards stay plain paper.
  - **Cards in 3D (2026-10-30):** a full-screen card (reveal, Collection pager, dev preview) is flat at rest and follows the finger while pressed and held (long press, so swipes between cards still work), with the boosters' perspective; no sway on its own (tried: nauseating). **Epic and Legendary cards are glossy:** a plastic-like white highlight band slides across them with the tilt (shown once a reveal's flip is done). Shared PNGs stay flat, without the shine. Test builds have a "Dev: preview a card reveal" row at the bottom of Credits (one chip per rarity; nothing saved).
  - **Quality %** = average percentile of the 4 rolls. Duplicates are kept (×N); the collection shows the best copy.
- **Card design:** portrait 5:7, flat solid frame in the rarity color (Legendary: thicker frame), photo, name in the app language + Latin, rarity label, 4 stats with a small bar, quality %, number `#042 / 272`. No gradients, no shadows, one radius (Dawn rules still apply). Cards are always printed on paper (ink text), whatever the app theme. The reveal screen *is* the card.
- **Collection tab** (UI rounds 3–4, 2026-10-29): views **All** (default, every copy) | **Species** (best copy, rarest first) | **one view per region**: the current region, then every other region a card was won in (2026-10-30), each with its own birds in 3 compact columns ("?" for undiscovered); counts inside the view menu ("All (15)", "France (9/353)", "USA — Northeast (2/500)"; another region's list is downloaded once to know its total, "(2)" until then; views are grouped by **displayed name**, so GPS cells sharing a name are one view with their lists combined); All/Species show the full card scaled down, 2 per row; rarity menu items in their rarity colors; "Series" button top right. Tap → full card, swipe between cards.
- **Bottom bar:** Twitch | **Alarms** (accent circle, the main action, ring when open) | Collection (small, muted until selected), on **frosted glass** over the pages (decided 2026-10-29 after a plain bar and a shaped one were rejected).
- **Twitch mode ("Cocheur", decided 2026-10-29):** a training ground that wins cards like alarms do. Up to 5 boosters, +1 every 15 min (10 until the 2026-10-31 balance pass) (derived from one timestamp; nothing runs in the background), countdown under the pile to the second. The **Twitch Zone** shows the boosters as a floating pile (drag = parallax tilt); **a tap opens one** (the top strip tears off) and starts the quiz: the alarm's 4 photos, the song looping in the app, "Listen again", back allowed (forfeits it). Never touches the wake-up streak. Same bird pick as alarms (season, difficulty, recent birds, songs on the phone).
- **Card reveal (decided 2026-10-29):** after a right answer (alarm or Twitch) the card **flips face up in place** (~0.55 s), then sparkles that **grow with the rarity**: Common a small burst behind the card; Uncommon/Rare + twinkling stars round the edge; Epic + orbiting glints; Legendary + a sheen across the card (1.1 s → 2.5 s in all). No "New card!" title. A wrong answer still shows the blurred "heard" card.
- **Series (decided 2026-10-29):** badges in pages of 2×3 (Collection → Series, small back button bottom right). Kinds: families (rules on order/family/genus), hand-picked themes, Birders' legends (iconic twitcher birds), and game achievements (Legendary hunter, Perfect week, Full set, Collector 100 cards, Local expert 50 region species, Rare finds 5 Rare+ — in the app's code). Source `pipeline/series.md` → `series.json` (~9 KB, published with the catalog and bundled as fallback; **no per-bird tagging**, the catalog's taxonomy does the work). A series shows only with ≥ 3 birds in the player's region; badge = the signature bird's photo (or the region's first). Completing one (once per region; achievements once) turns the ring foil and gives **+1 bonus Twitch booster**, on top of the 5 and used first, granted silently.
- **Sharing (moved into v1):** a share button turns the card into a PNG for the phone's share sheet (`share_plus`). No account, no server.
- **Reviewer polish (2026-11-01, a late reviewer's 7 comments, all taken):**
  - **Bird rarity row** on cards, below the name: 0.5–5 stars (half steps) = **10 levels** from the bird's share of the GBIF records in its commonest month, each tier split in two (`level()` in `pipeline/build.py`, cut-offs 3 % / 1 % / 0.5 % / 0.25 % / 0.11 % / 0.05 % / 0.022 % / 0.01 % / 0.003 %, ≥ 20 records for the last two). Stored as `"r"` in each region file; for the **current region** only (no row for a bird absent from it, or before the region file is refreshed). More stars = rarer. The photo box gives up the room (the blurred letterbox bands shrink). Stars 22 px; from 4 stars a light sweep and two twinkles, at 5 stars brighter with three sparkles orbiting the row.
  - **Expert difficulty:** all birds and vocalizations like Hard, **no choices: the name is typed** (matches in the app's language, English or Latin, among the region's birds, names only); Epic and Legendary card odds × 1.2 vs Hard (50.6 / 25.3 / 12.7 / 7.6 / 3.8 %). Made for a groggy morning: the bird's photo blurred past recognition (σ 30, under a 55 % veil of the theme) as background texture, a large frosted-glass field outlined in the accent a third of the way down, big match tiles (word-start matching); a tap closes the keyboard and frames the pick, checked 0.7 s later with the photo choices' animation.
  - **Sound effects: tried and removed** the same day (the user didn't like the sound design): 3 synthesized sounds (tap, page swish, reveal chime) and a Settings switch. The app has no sound effects; revisit only with real sound design.
  - **Full-screen collection card:** Wikipedia button (a serif "W"; the article in the app's language, by Latin name) beside Share / Info (a Merlin button was tried and removed); cards line up when swiping; the copy count as a large pill in the card's rarity colour, midway between the top and the card.
  - Trade / Series: text only, bold and larger. Settings headers bold.
  - Every screen keeps its buttons clear of the **3-button navigation bar** (tested in `a11y_test.dart`).
- **Not in v1:** trading between players (needs accounts + a server), any paid item. (Free boosters exist since 2026-10-29: they are the Twitch quizzes, never sold.)
- **Rarity from eBird when a region has enough of it (2026-11-03):** stars and tiers came from the share of all GBIF records, so one dedicated survey made a rare bird look common (the Hohe Tauern park database is 16,655 of Austria's 18,395 Bearded Vulture records: level 1, ½ star, in Austria South-West, and a "common" tier that rang it often). `month_counts()` now takes each region-month's rarity from eBird (checklist data, comparable across species) when it has ≥ 5,000 eBird records (`EBIRD_MIN`), else all data as before; presence still uses every dataset. Austria South-West: Bearded Vulture level 1 → 4, Golden Eagle 2 → 3, Robin and Great Tit stay 1. eBird is 3–24 % of records in Europe, 99 % in the US.
- **Cards keep their rarity stars (2026-11-03):** `CardData.level` is saved when the card is won (the bird's level in that region), like its rarity and region; older cards show the current region's stars, as before.
- **Permanent card numbers (2026-11-03):** `assign_numbers()` keeps each species' number from `pipeline/numbers.json` (started from the live 1,713-species catalog, gh-pages 35c2734); dropped species leave gaps, new ones get the next numbers in taxonomic order. Before, numbers were recomputed every build, so the NC-free catalog would have renumbered most players' cards.
- **5 s minimum recordings (2026-11-03):** iNaturalist clips ≥ 5 s; xeno-canto A/B/C ≥ 5 s only for a species with nothing ≥ 10 s, so existing species keep their recordings (checked: Blackbird, Robin, Great Tit, Teal, Swallow unchanged). GBIF as a third source measured and set aside: 121 dropped species have a strictly free sound, but 100 of them are Berlin Animal Sound Archive files whose GBIF links are dead (404) and short excerpts; only 4 of the 14 most widespread gave a usable clip.
- **Rarity odds lowered (2026-11-03, after extensive play: rare cards dropped too often):** `rarityScale` in `birds.dart` keeps a share of the decay curve for each tier above Common, Common takes the rest: ×0.8 on Medium and Hard; Easy ×0.95 for Uncommon and Rare (hook new players), ×0.8 above; Expert unchanged (reward experts). Odds C/U/R/E/L — Easy 76.7/17.9/4.5/0.9/0 %, Medium 72.3/18.3/6.4/2.2/0.8 %, Hard 61.3/20.6/10.3/5.2/2.6 %, Expert 50.6/25.3/12.7/7.6/3.8 %. The bird lean still applies on top (Hard: a common bird 88/9.9/1.6/0.3/0 %, a legendary one 11/11/16.5/25/37 %).

- Light: bg `#F6F1EA` · text `#1C1B1A` · accent `#E86A4F` · success `#3E8E6A` · fail `#C9503C`

Settings → Theme: a row of drawn birds in each theme's plumage on its surface color, with the palette as dots (Phone = a half-light, half-dark phone; default: Blue Tit by day, Blackbird in dark mode); old saved themes fall back to Phone. The selected option of every Settings choice is filled with the theme's accent; the others stay neutral. Success, fail and rarity colors are the same in every theme; cards are always printed on paper (the Dawn ink/paper/coral tokens).
- App ID `com.nathanprovin.earlybird` (decided 2026-10-28, permanent once on Google Play). Icon: coral half-sun on the horizon with an ink bird perched in front, on paper.
- Coral on paper is about 3:1 contrast, so use it only for large or graphic elements. Body text is always ink.
- Pop-up: a solid circle scales in (moss ✓ / brick ✕), with haptics and the streak count ticking up or resetting.

## Tips and Twitch Plus — decided 2026-11-03 (branch `adding-tips`)
- **Everything through Google Play Billing, inside the app** (`in_app_purchase`, `lib/purchases.dart`): Play requires its own billing even for tips, so no external "buy me a coffee" page or link.
- **No loot boxes:** nothing random is ever sold (no roulette, no paid boosters): no odds disclosure, no "random items" rating, no Belgian / Dutch restrictions.
- **Tips** (Settings → Support Early Bird → Buy me a coffee, `ui/tips.dart`): 4 consumable products `tip_espresso` 0.99 €, `tip_cappuccino` 1.99 €, `tip_breakfast` 4.99 €, `tip_binoculars` 19.99 € ("Stabilized binoculars", no brand: the 1,715 € joke is above Play's price cap). Pure thanks: nothing in the game changes; a thank-you dialog with the theme's bird. Prices always come from Play (localized), never hard-coded.
- **Twitch Plus** (`twitch_plus`, one-time 1.99 €, non-consumable; FR Cocheur Plus, DE Twitcher Plus, ES Pajarero Plus): a booster every **1 min** instead of 15 (`twitchPlusEvery`, `store.twitchPeriod`); buying it fills the pile at once (the pile derives from one timestamp). Offered by a bolt button bottom right of the Twitch Zone, only while the countdown runs; a "Plus" chip by the title once owned. Restored at each launch (`restorePurchases`) after a reinstall. A monthly 0.99 € plan was rejected: needs status checks, expiry and Play subscription disclosures for a small perk.
- **Trusted on the device** (no server check: `shortcut:` comment in purchases.dart). Without Google Play (emulator, tests) the pages say purchases are unavailable and every button is disabled.
- **Supporter theme postponed** (it would change the Settings layout).
- **Offline birds never NC:** `build_fallback()` ranks only species with a free recording and asserts the bundled one isn't NC (2026-11-03; 20 birds re-picked from the NC-free catalog, 0 NC).
- **Ships with the NC-free catalog** (pipeline `ALLOW_NC = False`, iNaturalist fallback): money in the app breaks the NC recording licences.

## Scaling — decided 2026-11-10
- **Bottleneck:** no accounts and no server. Fixed once, in this order: own domain in `baseUrl` (1.0.1, before Production) → catalog on **Cloudflare R2** behind it (no egress fees, changed files only) → **Firebase** Auth (anonymous + Google) + Firestore for per-player data. Firebase never hosts the catalog (download fees) and is never on the alarm's path (offline alarms).
- **Cards rolled on the phone until the marketplace;** then pre-rolled and signed by the server at deck download, revealed offline; older cards "pre-season".
- **Saved data only grows:** optional fields with defaults, keys never renamed, a test loads a 1.0.0+3 preferences file.
- **Region cells stay 2°** (50 km cells would be ~14× more cells, sparse GBIF data); the region UI gets reworked later instead.
- **Codes and referrals:** word codes (`RedRobin`…), once per account (Firestore create-only document), invite code in shared links via the Play Install Referrer; rewards still to decide.
- **Shiny = 6th tier above Legendary, card only** (never a bird tier), **1 / 500 per card** of a species with a Shiny photo (was 1 / 1000; 2026-11-11, user): about half the cards are of such species (leucism is photographed on common birds), so ~1 Shiny in 1,000 cards overall, one every ~4 months for an active player (~10 cards a day). 172 photos for 171 species picked by the user from contact sheets (`pipeline/shiny.csv`). Two looks (`ui/shiny.dart`): **leucistic** = diamond / ivory (faceted white frame, sparkles, blurred facets on the face), **melanistic** = obsidian (dark card, white text, blurred purple magma veins glowing through frame and face); both a knurled band with running lights, header "Shiny · <kind>", a 4.2 s firework reveal.
- **Card variants (2026-11-11):** up to 5 Commons Featured / Valued / Quality photos per species (2,633 for 778 species); a card gets one of the bird's photos at random (first tied to the rarity, best to Legendary: changed the same day) and saves it. **All photos 1200 px at high quality** (user: quality over size; catalog photos ~850 MB on R2, a region's first sync ~70 MB); catalog photo names carry the width (`-w1200`) so phones fetch sharper ones, and the app deletes photos the catalog no longer lists.
- **Play button** on cards: the disc is printed in the frame's material (cardboard … obsidian).
- **Balance after a 1,278-card simulation (2026-11-11, Hard, France · Nord: 59 / 25 / 10.5 / 3.4 / 2.0 / 0.08 %; Legendary only 1.65× rarer than Epic, 26 Legendaries on 17 species):** Legendary 20 % rarer on Medium / Hard / Expert (`rarityScale` 0.64 / 0.64 / 0.8; base odds Hard 61.8 / 20.6 / 10.3 / 5.2 / 2.1 %); a species already owned as Legendary (given cards count) has half the Legendary odds; a card's photo is any of the bird's photos (catalog or variant) at random, no longer tied to its rarity. Measured on a second 1,500-card simulation: 60.1 / 23.4 / 11.1 / 3.7 / **1.6** / 0.07 %, Epic 2.3× Legendary, at most 2 Legendary copies per species, 75 % variant photos. **Easy gets a tiny Legendary chance** (none before): `rarityScale` 8 on its Legendary step, a 2.3 % base that the Easy draft (Common / Uncommon birds only) and the 6× lean bring down to ~1 card in 6,000.
- **Odds fitted to play time (2026-11-11, user):** reference pace **16 cards a day** (1 alarm + 3 Training sessions of 5). **Shiny 1 / 1250** per eligible card: one every 5–7 months on every difficulty (same luck for all). **Hard** `rarityScale` [0.8, 0.466, 0.171, 0.077]: Rare ~10 %, an Epic every ~3 days, a Legendary every ~2 weeks; **Expert** [1.0, 0.581, 0.262, 0.166]: an Epic every ~2 days, a Legendary every ~week (fitted on France · Nord, average of November and May). Easy and Medium unchanged (Easy: Epic ~5 months, Legendary ~13 months; Medium: Epic ~1 week, Legendary ~3–4 months). Consequence: a Legendary-tier bird on Hard now gives 30 % Common, 8.5 % Legendary (it no longer peaks at its own tier, but still gives far more rare cards than a common bird).
- **The deck is a pile (2026-11-11, user):** 14 cards (fresh songs); each ring draws the **top** card (the one waiting longest), the freed slot gets a new bird drawn by rarity weight, put **under** the pile; cards of the pile are never deleted by the clean-up; each scheduled alarm holds its own card (two alarms, or an alarm and a booster, never share one). **Bug fixed:** rings used to pick *again* by weight among the deck's songs, so scarce birds' songs waited, aged past the 60 kept downloads and were deleted unplayed: Epic- and Legendary-tier birds rang at ~0.4× their share (measured on a 1,000-card simulation), hence fewer Rare / Epic cards than the odds. Verified on the phone (Hard, 1,000 cards): 61.5 / 26.6 / 9.5 / 1.8 / 0.6 %, bird tiers at their model share, 159 species. **Rest window (same day):** a played song stays out of the pile for the last 200 plays, or half the songs that can ring now if fewer (Easy has ~110 songs in a region: with 200 the pile ran dry after a week and the offline fallback replayed the same songs); a song leaving its rest goes under the pile. The pile is refilled before the region's photos are downloaded.
- **Photos: download once, keep what you own (2026-11-11, user):** no prefetch of variant / Shiny photos (all of a pile bird's photos came down, ~15 MB a day, though a card shows one); a won card's photo is fetched at the reveal (≤ 4 s, else the catalog photo shows until the next sync fetches it) and kept for good; each sync deletes every photo but the region's catalog photos and the owned cards' photos (other regions' birds not owned, old extras). ~5 MB a day at 16 cards; space ~150–200 MB after a year in one region, ~400 MB realistic after years (10k cards, several regions), 850 MB ceiling (every photo of the catalog). Measured before the change: 406 MB of photos after the simulations (252 MB of variants). Rarity odds differ by region (California: a Legendary every ~5 days on Hard vs ~2 weeks in France): kept, no per-region scaling (user). **The card's photo is picked when the bird is drawn** and downloads during the quiz (the reveal waits for the same download only if needed, 4 s max). ## Tech choices
- Flutter packages: `alarm`, `geolocator` (coarse), `http`, `path_provider`, `shared_preferences`, `flutter_localizations` (ARB en/fr), `just_audio` (card ▶ and the Twitch quiz song), `share_plus`, `url_launcher`, `permission_handler`. State: built-in `ChangeNotifier`. No package for animations, sensors or app info: boosters, flip and sparkles are plain Flutter (Transform, CustomPainter); the app version comes from a tiny Android channel (`early_bird/version` in MainActivity.kt).
- Testing on the S22: `flutter build apk --profile` (near-release speed, same debug signature as debug builds, so data is kept) and `adb install --no-streaming -r` (streamed installs of the 107 MB APK fail on this USB link). Unit/widget tests in `app/test/widget_test.dart` cover the bird pick, cards, stats formats, Twitch maths, series rules, booster tap, the flip.
- Content pipeline (`pipeline/build.py`, Python stdlib + ffmpeg, run offline): GBIF occurrences per 2°×2° cell × month (presence + rarity) and GBIF taxonomy (order/family) + Wikidata EN/FR names and photo (GBIF vernacular names as fallback; replaces the IOC spreadsheet, decided 2026-10-27) + Wikimedia Commons photos (CC0/PD/BY/BY-SA; GFDL rejected) + xeno-canto v3 (API key in git-ignored `pipeline/xc_key.txt`; CC0/BY/BY-SA only, quality A/B, 30s clips normalized to −16 LUFS, AAC 48 kbps .m4a). Your own photos: `pipeline/my_photos/<Latin_name>.jpg` (+ optional `credits.csv`); manual picks: `pipeline/overrides.csv`.
- Each ring gets its bird when scheduled (the `alarm` package needs a local file). If the file is missing, a bundled fallback bird plays, so the alarm always rings.

- **Rarity lean toward the bird (2026-10-31, replaces the flat tilt of 2):** each step between a card's rarity and its bird's tier divides the odds by `rarityLean / rarityDecay` = 1.5 / r (Easy 6, Medium 4.3, Hard 3) — stronger than the decay, so a scarce bird's cards peak at its own tier ("a rare bird gives a rare card"): on Hard a Legendary bird gives 7.6 / 11 / 17 / 26 / 38 %, a common bird 83 / 14 / 2.3 / 0.4 / 0.1 %.
