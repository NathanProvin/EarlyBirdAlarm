Early Bird Alarm — Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026.

Early Bird Alarm is an alarm clock that plays a bird song and lets you guess the bird. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal data.

Location

If you choose "Use my location", the app asks Android for your approximate (city-level) position once, to pick the birds of your area. The position is used only on your phone to choose a region and is never sent anywhere. You can instead pick a country from a list.

Downloads

Bird names, photos and recordings are downloaded from GitHub Pages (nathanprovin.github.io). As with any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request information; see GitHub's privacy statement. The app sends no other data.

Your collection and backup

Your alarms, settings and card collection are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android copies them to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup), so they come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. We cannot see this backup.

Permissions

  • Alarms, notifications, full-screen display and wake lock: to ring and show the bird quiz, even when the phone is locked.
  • Approximate location: only if you choose "Use my location" (see above).
  • Internet: to download the birds of your region.
  • Bluetooth, nearby devices and precise location: only when you open Trade (see below).

Face-to-face trades

When you open Trade, your phone looks for another phone running the app nearby, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi (Google Nearby Connections). Android requires the Bluetooth, nearby-devices and precise-location permissions for this; your position is neither used nor sent. Only the cards you choose to trade go directly from one phone to the other; nothing passes through a server.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
