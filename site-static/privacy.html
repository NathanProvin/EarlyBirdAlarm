Early Bird Alarm — Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Early Bird Alarm is an alarm clock that plays a bird song and lets you guess the bird. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal data. The only data we keep is the cloud backup of your card collection, described below.

Location

If you choose "Use my location", the app asks Android for your approximate (city-level) position once, to pick the birds of your area. The position is used only on your phone to choose a region and is never sent anywhere. You can instead pick a country from a list.

Downloads

Bird names, photos and recordings are downloaded from cdn.earlybirdalarm.app, hosted by Cloudflare (version 1.0.0 downloads them from GitHub Pages, nathanprovin.github.io). As with any website, the host receives your IP address and basic request information; see Cloudflare's and GitHub's privacy policies. The app sends no other data.

Your collection and backup

Your alarms and settings are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is on, Android also copies them to your own Google account (Android Auto Backup). We cannot see this backup.

Account and cloud backup (from version 1.1). At first launch the app creates an anonymous account (Firebase Authentication, by Google) and saves a copy of your card collection to it (cards, birds heard, series, streaks, bonus boosters) in Google Cloud Firestore, in the European Union. If you sign in with Google or with an email and password, we keep that email address (and, with Google, your name), so your collection comes back on any phone where you sign in. We use this data only for the backup. You can delete your account and its backup at any time in Account (the person icon at the top of Alarms and Training), or by following these steps; it is erased at once.

Invites and codes (from version 1.1). An invite link you share contains your anonymous account ID, so that Google Play can tell the app of a friend who installs it through the link. The friend's app then stores in Firestore which account invited it; you only see how many friends joined, never who. When you enter a code, the app stores that this account used it, so each code works once. Deleting your account deletes the record of who invited you; the record of a used code keeps only the deleted account's ID.

Profile and friends (from version 1.1). Your account gets a public @name (generated at first, changeable once a week). Your profile shows that name, a picture you crop from one of your own cards and the 3 cards you choose for your hall of fame; it is stored in Firestore and other signed-in players can see it when they look up your @name. Friend requests and friendships are stored in Firestore too; a friendship exists only once you accept a request. From a friend's profile you can remove them, block them (their requests are then hidden) or report their name to us by email. Names must be respectful: we remove offensive names and may delete the accounts behind them. Deleting your account deletes your profile, your @name, your friendships and your pending requests.

Permissions

  • Alarms, notifications, full-screen display and wake lock: to ring and show the bird quiz, even when the phone is locked.
  • Approximate location: only if you choose "Use my location" (see above).
  • Internet: to download the birds of your region.

Trades with friends

From version 1.1 you trade cards with your friends at a distance. A trade and the cards each of you offers are stored in Firestore and can be read only by the two of you; it is deleted when the swap is done, when one of you cancels it, or after 7 days. The cards you receive are checked by the app before they join your collection. (The earlier face-to-face trades over Bluetooth, and their Bluetooth, nearby-devices and precise-location permissions, are gone.)

Purchases

Tips and Twitch Plus are optional purchases processed by Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only learns which product was bought, to unlock Twitch Plus on your phone.

Children

The app collects no personal data beyond the account described above, and nothing specific to children.
